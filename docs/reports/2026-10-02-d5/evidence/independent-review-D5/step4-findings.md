## Fresh primary corroboration and limits

[Cloud Run autoscaling](https://docs.cloud.google.com/run/docs/about-instance-autoscaling) confirms CPU/concurrency-based behavior and queuing; [maximum instances](https://docs.cloud.google.com/run/docs/configuring/max-instances) does not create a hard physical spend cap. [Azure Container Apps reliability](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/reliability-container-apps) supports creation-time zone-redundancy conditions, while [Azure PostgreSQL reliability](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/reliability-database-postgresql) describes synchronous HA; exact Japan fit remains U. [RDS failover](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonRDS/latest/UserGuide/Concepts.MultiAZ.Failover.html) gives typical variable times, not a whole-service bound. [Cloud SQL HA](https://docs.cloud.google.com/sql/docs/postgres/high-availability) similarly gives prerequisites and approximate connection interruption.

[PostgreSQL role limits](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/sql-createrole.html) are approximately enforced and superuser-exempt; simultaneous connection admission can reject both, so no claim of overshoot is inferred from that source. [Reserved connection slots](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/runtime-config-connection.html) require managed-SKU headroom verification. [S3 replication](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonS3/latest/userguide/replication-what-is-isnot-replicated.html) requires explicit destination version deletion; [KMS deletion](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/kms/latest/developerguide/deleting-keys.html) makes pending keys unusable and completed deletion irreversible; [Azure role definitions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/role-based-access-control/role-definitions) confirms NotActions is not deny. The packet accounts for these caveats.

[Cloud Run pricing](https://cloud.google.com/run/pricing) supports the narrow Tokyo CPU/memory arithmetic; other prices and invoice completeness are not established.
