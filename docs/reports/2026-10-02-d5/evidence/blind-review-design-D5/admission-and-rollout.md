Native max replicas/instances are sizing controls, not guaranteed physical ceilings. At most8 serving capacity slots plus2 rollout slots is the proposed app capacity envelope. ## Hard logical permits

Application gate at every ingress handler obtains a globally unique request permit before business work. Proposed256 active permits across all revisions and replicas, plus at most32 globally waiting requests and20ms wait ceiling; inherited32 pending/replica is superseded. Every replica still caps32 active handlers. Fail to acquire by20ms: 429/Retry-After1s (record unavailable principal request); state/coordinator failure: fail closed without starting SQL/vendor work. AdmissionCoordinator is a conditional small private managed-app component using the HA SQL control store, not the edge rate limiter. Charge its replicas/runtime/SQL/pool/logs separately in C01/C02/C09. It authenticates approved digest/environment/instance identity and leases permits with epoch/fence tokens. Coordinator DB role≤4 connections uses the existing operations10 allocation (leaving6 for migration/operator). No PII/tokens in permit rows. It cannot approve IAM or data publication.

A TTL alone cannot safely reissue a permit while an old handler continues. Proposed lease2s, heartbeat0.5s and handler wall deadline450ms (measured from external entrance, including queue/network). On expiry stop new work; database mutating function rechecks permit epoch within the transaction. Do not reclaim a permit until predecessor completion/transaction abort is known; otherwise mark occupied and shed until resolved. Never restart epoch counters at zero after loss. Independent epoch integrity/transaction cancellation is a later validation predicate; if unavailable, global bound is UNVERIFIED and stop promotion/release. Coordinator unavailable reduces availability; preserve this dependency in monthly SLO and formal recovery evidence, no fail-open.

If managed reserved slots or lower max reduce user capacity, recalculate and lower app/role caps before adoption. Direct DB auth permission does not grant these SQL privileges.
