# Replay completeness after ledger loss and restore publication

Retention survival and disaster survival differ. The original hourly deletion manifest can omit the newest withdrawals and is superseded by a conditional append/replication protocol below. Allowed linkage, start event, exceptions, retention and recipient boundary remain unanswered; no42d ledger exception is assumed. The30d active/35d oldest-backup requirements remain fixed. Lack of approved permissible linkage may make replay impossible; do not solve by secretly extending retention or losing deletion evidence.

## Conditional journal and independent surviving copy

Each validated withdrawal request is assigned a monotonic journal sequence/epoch, case digest and approved minimal subject linkage in an atomic transaction with account-write withdrawal and pending copy work. Preserve request-received, validation/decision and completion times separately; do not shift the formal start to a later approval. Commit an independently protected append record or outbox durably before acknowledging a completed deletion case; exact withdrawal API acknowledgment/availability policy requires owner decision. Until independent receipt, account stays withdrawn, case remains pending and disaster-copy completeness cannot be claimed. An independent copy is in approved Japan protection boundary with separate access/key/audit and survives loss of primary ledger; other-region requirement/scope and priceU, not presumed approved. Same-region isolated copy protects credential/ledger loss but not region loss; cross-region approved copy needed for that disaster. Synchronous replication across sites adds latency/cost/availability sensitivity; async outbox means latest acknowledgment gap remains until receipts caught up.

Use hash-chained events with sequence, epoch, previous digest and per-class receipt-set digest; signatures independently validated. Minimal linkage can still be pseudonymous personal data; its permission/retention is explicitly owner-open. High-watermark certificates contain epoch, contiguous prefix length, digest and custody/receipt times. A timestamp alone, backup file existence or max(sequence) does not prove no holes. Reconcile pending cases, late third-party receipts and duplicate/reordered events; deterministic case execution permits retries without double deletion. Poll chain health every30s; first broken signature/gap/epoch rollback is immediate integrity incident, stale independent receipt>40s UNKNOWN. Snapshot/export catalog daily includes every ledger/audit replica/backup/export, key, location and expiry; independent usable replay coverage is verified separately from byte storage.
