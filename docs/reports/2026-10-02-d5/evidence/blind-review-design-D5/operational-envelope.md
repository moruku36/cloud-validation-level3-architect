# Proposed operational envelope and response clocks

Every number in this file is a conditional design proposal except explicit formal acceptance values. These values are not approved thresholds or observed capacity. Later authorized measurements must validate them; until then all SLO/RTO/RPO/performance conclusions remain UNVERIFIED.

DB configured/verified max connections 120 is conditional; a lower supported budget lowers app max before deployment. ## Unattended and staffed limits

Preapproved finite automation may replace stateless replicas, refresh pools to a verified fenced primary, retry idempotent queue work, roll back an approved digest, renew certs and enforce admission limits. It cannot approve data loss, grant IAM/key rights, choose a new geography, spend beyond approved envelope, publish a quarantined restore, purge audit evidence or infer deletion completion. Named authorities/approvals remain absent; proposed automation is disabled until those conditions and independent validation exist.
REQ-10 budget proposal: detection 65s + writer/zone isolation 120s + provider recovery/scale 600s + app/pool recovery 300s + external stability 300s + margin 415s=1800s. These are allocated budgets, not provider guarantees. At15m without confirmed recovery freeze repeated mutation; at25m without beginning five-minute stable window record likely deadline miss and await daytime authority. RPO watermark must remain≤300s. Monthly 99.9% budget is43.2 min in30d or44.64 min in31d, includes all formal dependencies/planned downtime; four 15m incidents would exceed it.
Cloud Run minimum instances do not give user-controlled AZ placement or guaranteed ready capacity; Container Apps and Fargate placement/quotas also require service-specific evidence. Warm 4 is a logical N+1 quantity, not proof of surviving two instances in a particular managed-runtime zone. Recompute replicas as ceil(target RPS/measured per-replica throughput), surviving-zone headroom, connection sum, request deadline and bill quantities whenever shapes/concurrency or measured throughput change. Do not increase max replicas without reducing pools or increasing a verified DB budget.
Logical restore allocation from authorized decision: fencing 15m, restore 90m, reconcile 45m, deletion replay/validation 60m, approval+publication 30m=240m; approval delay can consume the target. Overnight absent decision does not start the four-hour clock, but customer impact is separately counted in monthly SLO. Region cold rebuild proposed decision+24h has no approved objective and no automatic publication.
