## Adapter complexity sensitivity

Shared app adds readiness/idempotency/deletion/broker interfaces; each chosen provider needs exactly one runtime/network/identity/queue/key/monitoring adapter. Do not operate all three production stacks. Conditional monthly adapter review2–4h is included once in operations-accounting.md, not added three times. An admission service+broker deployment adds two managed app roles plus permit/receipt tables, readiness, one release manifest and audit paths; minimum-dev needs isolated synthetic equivalents. Avoid a new cache/etcd solely for permits by using managed HA SQL control tables, but that couples admission to DB health and produces fail-closed outage. A separate HA coordinator could reduce that dependency but adds storage/compute/network/operations and quoteU; not adopted without evidence. DirectVPC→connector, private-endpoint→gateway and custom-controller alternatives add meter/resource count and2–4h maintenance sensitivity. Broad network isolation or unsupported native controls may justify managed VM/Kubernetes alternative only after full labor/quote review; Kubernetes remains an initial rejection with explicit revisit, not a predetermined answer.
